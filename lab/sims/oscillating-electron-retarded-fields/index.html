A MAP, NOT A MATERIAL

What the light means

The rings are instantaneous magnetic-field guides around the drive axis, sampled from the Liénard–Wiechert solution. Their radii stay fixed as signs and strengths change. Neither guides nor arrows are particles, photons, or an electron’s structure.

Brightness b = min(1, log(1 + 120 g |B|) / log 13); ring opacity = 0.08 + 0.82 b. Zero fields hide their guides. E and S arrows use the same compression with factors 15 and 600, respectively; arrow lengths and brightness clip. Gain g and sampling alter only the drawing. Probe values are uncompressed totals and both contributions.

THE MODEL’S BOUNDARY

Prescribed classical motion

The external drive supplies the energy. Radiation reaction, quantum structure and the driving apparatus’s own fields are omitted. This is a single net charge, not a neutral dipole. The exact field includes velocity and acceleration terms; “radiation only” isolates the acceleration term.

Dimensionless length c/ω, time 1/ω; E unit |e|ω²/(4πε₀c²), B unit E/c, power unit e²ω²/(4πε₀c). Derivation, checks and limitations ↗ · Historical illustration ↗
